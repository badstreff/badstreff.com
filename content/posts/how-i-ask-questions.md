- Why ask questions
  - To get information :D
  - They help clarify ambiguity
  - Check your own understanding
  - they give others a chance to explain their thought process
  - Asking questions is a low risk way to introduce yourself
  - They give you an opportunity to express interest in work others are doing
    - And from HtWFIP this is a great geniune way to make them feel important

- What makes a good question?
  - A question is not for you to answer to make yourself feel smarter in a 'gotch ya' way...
  - A good question should make everyone in the room smarter
  - Sometimes it helps to ask a question you already know the answer to if I feel the topic or answer is important to state aloud 

- How to ask a question
  - Consider the audience and their authoritative-ness on the answers they could give
  - Clarify what you think the definition of a thing is if there is ambiguity
  - State what you know or believe to be true up to now
    - this gives someone an opportunity to interject and correct any assumptions
  - Ask the question
  - Ask a person directly

A lot of this assumes asking in-person / meeting - if you are using slack just fire of the question with a little context, async communication is a wonderful thing
