## Introduction

My personal thoughts from listening to the Audiobook [How to Win Friends & Influence People](https://www.audible.com/pd/How-to-Win-Friends-Influence-People-Audiobook/B002V5BV96?source_code=ASSGB149080119000H&share_location=pdp)

### Chapter 1

One of the things I can already tell that I like about the book are the war stories told alongside the advice. If I could summerize chapter 1 into a single sentence it would be: "don't criticize others". There are a few different points made around this chapter but the few that stick with me are the following

1. People generally do not view themselves as doing anything wrong and will likely double down when criticized and attempt to rationalize and defend their actions
2. People will only do what they want to do, you can certainly make them do something when you are in a position of authority but this only goes so far
3. Critisism _can_ breed resentment
4. Try to be empathic to how other make their decisions, put myself in their shoes

I feel a lot of this advice does not really apply to me in a professional setting, however in my personal life I do not do as good of a job practicing the above and will try to do better.
